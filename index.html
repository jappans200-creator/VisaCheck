Why I built this

In 2026, I walked into the Cyprus embassy with every document they asked for. My Irish permit had more than 3 months left. I had done everything right. I paid €90 and waited.

The rejection letter arrived with no explanation. But as the consular officer handed it back, he hinted at the real reason — they wanted 6 months of permit validity remaining, not 3. A rule that existed nowhere on their official website. A rule I only found buried in Reddit threads and YouTube comments after I had already lost my money.

That €90 hurt. But what hurt more was knowing it was completely avoidable. The information existed. It was just scattered across forums that take hours to find. So I built VisaCheck. Because no immigrant should lose money on a rejection they could have seen coming.
